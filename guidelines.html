🛡️ Safe Exchange Protocols

How Proof of Ownership Works

CampusFind prevents dishonest claims by requiring specific, non-public details before any lost item is handed over. Here is the recommended protocol.

1

Keep Secrets Hidden

When uploading a found item, do not post photos of serial numbers, lock screens, exact coin counts, or inside pockets. Keep those details as test questions.

2

Submit Verifiable Proof

Claimants must describe matching scratches, Bluetooth device names, sticker sets, purchase receipts, or unlock the device in front of the finder.

3

Meet in Public Zones

Never hand over valuable items in private or remote dorms. Only hand over items in monitored campus hubs like the Library Desk or Security Desk.

Valid Proof Examples by Category

📱 Smartphones, Tablets & Laptops

Valid Proof: Unlocking the screen passcode or fingerprint in person, stating the exact wallpaper image, or matching the IMEI / serial number found on the original purchase box or bill.

💳 Wallets, Purses & IDs

Valid Proof: Stating the student ID number or name on cards inside before seeing them, describing unusual contents (family photo, specific currency note, library cards).

🎧 Headphones, AirPods & Calculators

Valid Proof: Pairing automatically to the claimant's phone, showing the Bluetooth name (e.g., "Aarav's AirPods"), or naming specific stickers, engravings, or battery cover markings.

🔑 Keys & Personal Accessories

Valid Proof: Number of keys, specific brand names on keys, description of keychains, lanyards, or gym tags.
